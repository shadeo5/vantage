# Vantage — Design Brief (high-fidelity mockups)

*Paste this into Claude Design. Attach the v1 wireframes for structure and the original `design_handoff_vantage` for visual DNA.*

## Product in one line
Vantage gives a photographer a personal reason to shoot **today** — the right place, at the right light, matched to the gear in their bag. It's an **inspiration engine, not a utility**: it nudges you out the door.

## Who it's for
Active local photography enthusiasts — people who already shoot sometimes and want more/better reasons to get out in their own city. (Long-term mission: reactivating people whose cameras are gathering dust — so the feel should be **inviting to a lapsing shooter, never pro-gatekeeping or intimidating**.)

## The one idea to express
A single, confident **"here's your shoot today,"** personalized to your kit — with a ranked list of other options beneath it. Not a weather forecast, not a searchable spot database. **Lead with inspiration; keep utility (scores, data) in the background.** Positioning line: *own the life, not the light.*

## Visual identity — evolve, don't replace
Build on the existing **Vantage** system (see `design_handoff_vantage`): dark, editorial, photography-forward.
- **Palette:** dark canvas `#0F0F11`, warm ink `#F4F1EA`, golden-hour accent `#E9B872` (bright `#F2A93B`), blue-hour `#7FA0CF`; light-type colors (golden/blue/flat) as accents.
- **Type:** Newsreader (serif) for display / headlines / place names; Hanken Grotesk (sans) for UI / body.
- **Feel:** cinematic golden-hour, editorial, warm.
- **The shift from the original:** the old design was a *landscape scouting utility* — scores, badges, data. This is an *inspiration engine*. **Less scoring/utility, more warmth, invitation, and real photography.** Example shots should feel central, not decorative.

## Tone / voice
Inspiring, personal, invitational. *"Golden light at 8:15 — grab your 35mm and go."* Never clinical, never gatekeeping.

## Screens to design
Bottom tabs: **Today · Plan · Bag**

1. **Today (home) — the core.**
   - Dismissible "add your gear" banner at top (first-run state).
   - **One hero suggestion:** example photo · an inspiring one-line reason ("Sweet Auburn is alive tonight") · why it fits your gear ("perfect for your 35mm") · key facts (distance, light window, what's happening) · a primary **"I'm going"** action · a tap-to-reveal **"why this?"** (light + activity + gear).
   - Below the hero: a ranked **"Best near you"** list of the other options tonight.

2. **Spot detail (discovery) — the come-back / teaching layer.**
   - Example-photo hero · name + type · "why here" · the **light-quality curve** (a ramp peaking at golden hour) · **"what to look for here"** (concrete field notes — a mural, a corner, a crowd) · **"what people shoot here"** (real example frames) · getting there · "I'm going." Make it rich and inspiring.

3. **Gear onboarding ("what's in your bag?") — light, skippable.**
   - Add camera(s) and lenses/focal lengths, with a "pick your style instead" escape hatch. Lead with the payoff: *"so we tell you which lens to grab."* Setup step 2 of 3.

4. **Plan (This week) — the look-ahead slate.**
   - Upcoming shoot opportunities grouped by day: place · what's happening · light · gear-fit · an **"I'm going"** commit that builds **"Your plan."** Mix of light-, activity-, and event-driven reasons.

**Bonus hero moment:** the **lock-screen push notification** — *"Great evening to shoot — grab the 50mm. Golden hour hits 8:15 and Sweet Auburn is buzzing. 6 min away."*

## Do / Don't
- **Do:** lead with one confident, inspiring suggestion; make gear/personalization visible; use real photography prominently; warm golden-hour editorial feel.
- **Don't:** look like a weather dashboard; bury the screen in scores/data/badges; feel clinical, technical, or pro-gatekeeping.
