# Vantage — Voice & Tone

*The one-page guide for everything Vantage says. If you're writing a spot, a nudge, a button, or an empty state — it should sound like this. (This is the same voice baked into `content-pipeline/generate.mjs`; keep the two in sync.)*

## Who's talking
**A local photographer friend** texting you where to go tonight — someone who actually shoots this city, knows the corners, and wants you out the door with your camera. Not a brand. Not a weather app. Not a tour guide.

## Who's listening
An **enthusiast who already shoots sometimes** and wants a reason to do it more. They know what golden hour is. They don't need convincing that photography is fun — they need a specific, exciting *where* and *why* for tonight. (Long-term we're also inviting back people whose gear is gathering dust — so never gatekeep or intimidate.)

## The one line
**"Own the life, not the light."** We lead with inspiration; utility (scores, data) stays in the background.

## Six principles

1. **Concrete over generic.** "Backlit murals on Auburn Ave, glowing right at dusk" — never "great photo opportunities." Name the street, the moment, the frame.
2. **Personal & present.** Second person, present tense, active voice. "Traffic trails build after 8:15," not "traffic trails can be observed."
3. **Warm, not flowery.** Inviting and human, but no purple prose. One vivid image beats three adjectives.
4. **Inspire, don't sell.** Never salesy, never hype. The excitement comes from the specific detail, not exclamation marks.
5. **Never gatekeep.** No pro-snobbery, no jargon walls. A lapsing shooter should feel welcomed, not tested.
6. **Honest.** Don't invent precise facts you can't be sure of (exact hours, "last entry," prices). Keep logistics general. Never let copy or imagery misrepresent a real place.

## Micro-rules
- **Row reason:** ≤ ~7 words, a hook. *"Neon and spray paint after dark."*
- **Tagline:** one line under a title, sets the scene. *"The classic downtown view."*
- **Why:** 2–3 sentences — the reason to *go*, grounded in real detail.
- **What to look for:** exactly 3 tips, each a specific frame or moment.
- **Buttons/system copy:** plain and human. "Skip — I'll add later," not "Dismiss."

## Do / Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Low sun raking down Auburn Avenue" | "Beautiful lighting conditions" |
| "The Sunday market crowd fills the strip" | "High foot-traffic area" |
| "Grab a tele" | "Telephoto lens recommended" |
| "It never misses" | "Guaranteed stunning results!" |

## Gold standard (from the current app)
> *"The heart of Atlanta's historic Black business district hums on a warm evening — market stalls, front-porch talk, and low sun raking down Auburn Avenue. It's a street room where everyone's a little more open."*

That's the bar: specific, warm, present, a reason to go. Match it.
