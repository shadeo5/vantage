Who we're really for

We're not serving intent. We're creating it.

Most tools help people who've already decided to shoot. Our hero hasn't decided yet — they own a camera and haven't touched it in months. The whole product is the spark, not the map.

What the old design assumed

A search tool for photographers already deciding where to go.

Home = a ranked list of spots to browse. Utility. Serves existing intent.

What we're actually building

An inspiration engine that reaches out first.

Home = one personal, timely reason to shoot today, matched to your kit. It creates intent.

Principles

Four commitments that keep this honest.

1
Inspire, don't nag.
We stay silent unless today is genuinely good. A high bar to send is what separates a muse from spam.
2
Personal to the bag.
The suggestion knows your gear. A telephoto gets crowds and courts; a fast prime gets low-light streets and portraits. Your kit shapes the shoot.
3
We reach out.
The magic is proactive. The user shouldn't have to remember us — we remember them, at the right moment.
4
Lower the barrier, always.
Every choice removes friction between “camera in a drawer” and “out shooting.” Timing, place, subject — pre-answered.
What this means for the design

The current screens serve an older idea. Here's what has to change.

This section doubles as the redesign brief — the vision above, translated into what the app must become.

The home screen's job flips
From “browse 15 ranked areas” to “here's your one reason to shoot today” — a single, personal, hero-led answer. The list becomes backup below the fold, not the point.
Gear becomes a first-class citizen
A warm “what's in your bag?” onboarding, a kit profile, and visible influence — the app should show that a suggestion was chosen for your lens.
Tone shifts from utility to inspiration
Less scoring, badges, and density counts; more voice, story, and invitation. Copy that makes you want to grab the camera.
The notification is a designed surface
The push, the lock-screen moment, and the “here's why” it opens into are core screens — not an afterthought bolted on at the end.
Photography returns to a photography app
Example shots — what people actually captured here — carry the inspiration. Sourcing real imagery moves from “later” toward “core.”
The path there

Chase the notification's brain before its plumbing.

Prove the magic in-app first — a “Your shoot today” card — before building gear onboarding, a server, and push. Every step is something you can feel.
