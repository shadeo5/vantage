Never Say "Not Now"

The scorer went genre-honest (CB7) — but the surfaces the photographer actually reads still treat golden hour as the bar. At 9:30pm the hero cheerfully announces a golden window that closed an hour ago, and a quiet night goes blank. This ADR makes the hero and the detail page phase-honest: light is always the light you have now, never a window you missed.

01

The problem — the surfaces never got the CB7 memo

CB7 taught the scorer that golden hour isn't the bar. But two of the most-read surfaces still assume it is, and they contradict the shoot brief sitting inches away.

Bug — golden hour at 9:30pm

The hero lede and chip are hardcoded to the evening golden window and never check whether it's passed (InspirationHero.tsx:53,60 → goldenWindowLabel(windows), always goldenEvening.start–end). At 9:34pm in Atlanta — golden gone ~8:40 — it still reads "Golden hour hits 8:05 — grab your lens and go." It's telling you to catch a window that closed an hour ago.

The tell: the ShootBriefCard directly below it is already correct — App.tsx:169 reads shootAt = max(now, goldenEvening.start), so conditionsFor(now) returns night and the brief says "neon, light-trails, brace on a rail." The app knows it's night in one card and denies it in the card above.

Blank — the quiet night goes dark

bestNearYou() is quality-gated and returns [] on a slow night, so the whole list hides (App.tsx:257). The app goes quiet exactly when its job is to inspire — leaving the ShootBriefCard as generic, place-less advice ("brace on a rail" — which rail?).

Both are the same disease: golden-first, and mute after dark. The cure is one principle applied across the surfaces.

02

The principle — light is an asset you have, never a window you missed

This is the time-analogue of the app's oldest rule — gear is framed as ideal-for, never a limit (see VOICE.md). Applied to light:

The rule

Name the current light as something to shoot with. If a better window is still catchable today, surface it with a time and how long you've got. Once the good windows are behind you for the day, stop counting down — affirm the night. Never mourn light that's passed.

The whole light vocabulary flips from negative-and-past to positive-and-present. Flat light matters most here — it's the default state whenever the app opens in daytime, and the light street shooters most often dismiss. CB7 already proved it's a real creative mode; the copy has to say so.

Light now✗ Never✓ Instead
Flat · clear (harsh midday)"harsh light / bad light / wait for golden""Hard light — shoot the shadows, go graphic B&W"
Flat · overcast"flat / gray / dull""Soft even light — color pops, no harsh shadows"
Golden · now—"Golden hour — till 8:40, go"
Golden · ahead"not golden yet""Golden at 8:05 — 31 min out"
Blue · now—"Blue hour — sky and signs balance till 9:14"
Full night"blue closed 9:14 / golden gone 8:40 / not now""Night — the city's lit and yours"
The trap to avoid

Don't let the countdown demote the present. "Harsh now — but golden at 8:05" quietly says the light you have is wrong, wait. The current-light line stands on its own as a real option; the golden countdown rides alongside it, not as a correction to it.

03

The light module — forward-looking, three states

The detail chart today draws the full astronomical day (hourlyLight, 5am–9pm). At night that strands "now" at the far-right edge with everything interesting behind it — which is what makes the app read "not now." Fix the window and the negativity mostly dissolves: anchor at now, look forward ~6h. The module then adapts to what's actually ahead.

StateWhenShows
Light aheadFlat daytime — golden still comingCurrent-light line + forward mini-curve ramping to golden + countdown ("Golden in 31 min"). The money view.
In the windowGolden or blue in progressName it and count it down ("Blue — till 9:14"). Curve centered on the live peak.
Full nightNo catchable golden/blue left todayLine + morning peek — one positive status line, plus a small forward curve of tomorrow's golden ramp ("next golden 6:31a") for the early risers. No chart of dead dark.

Biggest when there's light to chase, quietest when there isn't — the right energy. The engine already supports it: hourlyLight() takes startHour/endHour, so the forward window is just feeding it now → now+6h; the state check is "any golden/blue bars ahead today?"

Placement — Option A, the compact strip (see the wires)

On the detail page the module is a slim strip, not the full-width headline it is today: one glanceable label + a mini sparkline. Light stays visible and useful; it stops being the throne. (Options B "collapse-on-tap" and C "fold into the brief" were considered — see §06.)

04

The detail page — re-ordered around "now"

The new body leads with place, then tonight, then the evergreen craft. The location prose stays — it's the soul of the spot.

#BlockStatus
1Hero — name, tagline, chips (phase chip, not a stale golden time)Adjust
2Why — the place's character, proseKeep
3What to shoot now — time-aware brief, the new body headlineNew
4Light strip — demoted, forward, phase-honest (§03)Demote
5See it on Street View — grounds the illustrated imageryNew

"What to shoot now" reuses the existing engine — shootBrief(now, thisSpot, kit, cloud) from lib/shootBrief.ts, already powering the Today hero — just fed now and this spot. Street View is a link off spot.lat/lon (maps.google.com/?q=&layer=c&cbll={lat},{lon}); because our imagery is deliberately illustrated (ILLUSTRATION_STYLE), it's the one place a scout can see the real geography. It leaves the app to Google Maps — an accepted trade for scouting value.

05

The quiet night — "Good in the dark," not a blank

When bestNearYou comes back empty, don't hide the section — swap it for evergreen spots whose window works right now, gated by what the kit can handle. The photographer's own example: motion-blur on a bridge, neon districts, a lit skyline.

  • Source — the same pack, filtered to spots live in the current phase (lightPhaseAt / windowType), ranked by kit-fit via the existing kitReadiness.
  • Honesty preserved — this isn't padding the "best near you" list with weak picks; it's a different, explicitly-labeled shelf ("Good in the dark") that only appears after hours. The quality gate on the ranked list stays intact.
  • Anchors the brief — with a spot attached, the generic "brace on a rail" becomes "brace on the Jackson Street Bridge rail."
06

Options considered

Light placement on the detail page (wired as A/B/C — see detail-light-wires):

OptionVerdictWhy
A · Compact stripChosenKeeps a real light signal — a shooter does want to know how long the light holds — but strips its false primacy. Smallest change that honors CB7.
B · Collapse-on-tapRejectedAdds a tap without adding clarity. The point is that light shouldn't cost the reader a decision.
C · Fold into the briefFallbackMost aggressive demotion — light becomes one status line, chart lives only on Today. Right call only if the per-spot chart proves redundant once A ships.

Full-night module behaviour: chosen line + morning peek over collapse-to-a-line-only (loses the early-riser planning aid) and always-show-the-curve (a flat dark chart is "nothing here" — the thing we're removing).

07

Consequences & what it touches

  • Resolves the hero/brief incoherence — both surfaces finally read the current phase. The 9:30pm bug is gone by construction.
  • A new voice rule lands in VOICE.md ("Light is an asset, never a miss") — enforced everywhere light is named: hero, detail, push copy (nudgeCopy.ts), the light-chart caption.
  • Mostly reuse, little new logic — shootBrief, conditionsFor, hourlyLight, goldenCountdown, kitReadiness all exist. The work is wiring + copy + a forward-window arg + one new "Good in the dark" filter.
  • Touches InspirationHero.tsx, SpotDetail.tsx, LightChart.tsx, App.tsx, lib/light.ts (forward window + phase-honest labels), a small lib/nudge.ts filter, and nudgeCopy.ts for the push.
  • The nightly push should get the same treatment so a 10pm nudge never says "golden at 8:05" — folds into the existing P3 follow-up (port the honest read into the Edge Function).
08

Open questions

  • The forward-window length — 6h is the starting point. Long enough to show an afternoon ramp into golden+blue; tune against how it feels.
  • "Good in the dark" ranking — pure kit-fit, or fold in variety/recency like the main list? Start simple (phase-live × kit-fit), earn more later.
  • Street View coverage — some spots (interiors, trailheads) have no panorama. Fall back to a map pin, or hide the link when coverage is unknown? (No cheap way to check coverage client-side — likely just label it "Map" when in doubt.)
  • Morning-peek reach — does the night module also mention tonight's next real opportunity if it's within a few hours (late blue, moonrise), or strictly tomorrow's golden?
