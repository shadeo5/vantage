Conditions & the Shoot Brief

Now that weather is a live signal, the nudge can stop being a go/no-go score and become a brief: given tonight's light, sky, and rain — plus your kit — here's what's shootable, whether your gear's up for it, and the shots to chase. Bad golden hour stops meaning "quiet night" and starts meaning "shoot this, this way."

Doc ADR — conditions guidance Status Additive brief shipped (CB1–4) · re-rank deferred Updated Jul 16, 2026
01

Why this earns its own layer

The nudge brain today answers one question — "is tonight worth going out?" — with a single score and a go/quiet verdict. The weather layer (P3, lib/weather.ts) just made that score honest about the sky. But it exposed a gap: on a 96% overcast evening the top spot's score drops just under the go bar, the verdict flips to "a quiet night," and — because "best near you" is gated on the same bar — the whole browse list vanishes. The app goes dark exactly when it should be most useful.

That's the wrong answer, because overcast isn't "nothing to shoot" — it's the wrong light for golden hour and the right light for even street, portraits, texture, and color. The fix isn't a looser threshold; it's a richer output. Turn the verdict into a shoot brief: a constructive read that always has a next move, even on a gray night or after dark.

The reframe in one line

From "Is tonight good? (score → go / quiet)" to "Given these conditions and your kit — shoot this, here's whether your gear's ready, here's the shot." Same signals, a brief instead of a gate.

It also lands squarely on the product's spine: a personal reason to go shoot. "Your f/1.8 eats this dark — go work the neon" is about the least generic thing the app can say, and it's ideal-for, never a limit — the standing gear-framing rule. This is the layer that makes weather + gear pay off together instead of as two separate multipliers.

Light phase and cloud already exist — light.ts gives the phase from sun altitude, weather.ts gives hourly cloud from Open-Meteo. The only new plumbing is precipitation, and it's cheap: the same Open-Meteo call, two more hourly variables.

// weather.ts — one-line extension of the existing fetch
hourly=cloud_cover,precipitation_probability,weather_code

// the normalized read the brief consumes
Conditions {
  phase: "golden" | "blue" | "flat" | "night"
  cloud: number   // 0..1, already have
  rain:  number   // 0..1 probability at the window (new)
  wet:   boolean  // weather_code says actively raining/snowing (new)
}
Honesty guardrail

We infer conditions from sun altitude + cloud + precip — we never meter the actual scene. So the brief says "looks like a fast-glass night," never "it's 3 lux." Same discipline as the rest of the brain: confident where the signal is real, quiet where it isn't.

03

Gear-readiness — is your kit up for tonight?

This is the half that's buildable today: every lens and body in gear.ts already carries maxAperture, so we can read the kit's fastest available f-number against the darkness and say something true. It's a pure function — kit + phase → a readiness verdict + an honest line.

That single table is the "fast lens / flash / chase the light / silhouette" fork from the brief — not four features, one readiness function reading aperture against darkness. Every branch stays framed as what the kit's ideal for and how to make the night work — it never tells someone their gear "can't."

Rain — the phased call

v1 (warn-first): when rain probability at go-time is high, the brief carries a plain nudge — "Rain likely at 8pm — make sure your body + lens are sealed, or pack a cover." No kit modeling yet. v2 (personalize): add a weatherSealed flag to the catalog (the "WR" / "GM" / "L" in model names already hints it) so the warning knows whether your gear is rated, and softens or sharpens accordingly.

04

Shot-type guidance — what to shoot

The creative payoff, and the honest answer to "quiet night." A rules table maps conditions → a handful of shot archetypes — the same spirit as the light-phase mapping we already have, and it reuses a precedent: the events data already carries kitAngles (wide / normal / tele / macro) and every spot a look[]. This makes that guidance conditional instead of static.

Guidance is filtered by the kit: "flash portraits" only surfaces if there's flash in the bag (later — we don't model flash yet); "light trails (tripod)" is offered as a technique, not assumed gear. Cross-checked against VOICE — specific, honest, never a generic "there'll be people there."

05

The decisions

Four forks sit inside this layer — which is why it earns an ADR. Recommendations are the flagged default calls; the load-bearing one is D1.

DecisionStatusReasoning / alternatives
D1 · Additive brief vs. conditions re-rank Open — your call The load-bearing one. Additive: keep the existing pick, add a readiness line + shot prompts beneath it (safe, ships alone, doesn't touch ranking). Re-rank: conditions change the hero — a gray evening promotes a flat-light spot over a washed-out golden one. Leaning: ship additive first, let it earn the re-rank. Re-rank overlaps the one-scorer principle in the Events ADR — do it there, not as a parallel system.
D2 · Rules table vs. LLM guidance Rules first Deterministic condition→shot rules are transparent, unit-testable, free, and match how the rest of the brain works. An LLM pass (like the events/spot draft) could tailor prompts per spot × condition later — escalate only if the rules feel generic. Rules for v1.
D3 · Weather-sealing Warn-first Your call, and the right one: warn on rain now (no kit data needed), add a structured weatherSealed flag to the catalog later to personalize. Ships value on day one; avoids a data pass across ~40 items before the feature's proven.
D4 · Readiness thresholds Tunable The f-stop cutoffs (≤2.0 set, >4.0 slow) are a first honest guess — they ignore body high-ISO ability (a modern sensor at f/4 can beat an old one at f/2). Single-constant knobs, tuned once we shoot against them. See simplifications.
Cross-reference

D1's re-rank touches the same scorer as the Gear Matching (Decision C) and Events ADRs. The rule holds: conditions influence one ranker, never a second parallel brain. The additive brief is safe precisely because it sits beside the ranker, not inside it.

06

Known simplifications & honesty guardrails

  • Inferred, not metered. Conditions come from sun altitude + cloud + precip, not a light meter on the scene. The brief hedges accordingly and never states a lux value.
  • Body ISO isn't modeled (D4). Readiness reads lens aperture only; a modern high-ISO body genuinely changes what "too slow" means. Documented so a slow-lens warning is never mistaken for a hard limit.
  • No flash / tripod in the kit data. Flash-lit and long-exposure prompts are offered as techniques, not assumed gear — until the catalog models accessories, we suggest, we don't assert you have them.
  • Ideal-for, never a limit. Every readiness branch — even "> f/4.0, slow for dark" — is framed as how to make the night work (last light / tripod / silhouette), never as gear that "can't." The standing rule, enforced in copy.
  • Rain warning is generic in v1. It tells everyone to check sealing regardless of their actual gear — honest but blunt, until weatherSealed lands (D3 v2).
07

Open questions

  • Does the brief ever re-rank the hero on conditions, or only annotate the existing pick? (D1 — needs the ranking model settled, overlaps Events.)
  • How many shot prompts is the right number — one hero shot, or a short set? Too many turns inspiration back into a checklist.
  • Where does the brief live in the UI — folded into "Why this pick?", a new "Tonight's shoot" card, or the spot detail's "what to look for"?
  • Should a genuinely bad night (dark + slow kit + rain, nothing sealed) still push a brief, or is "stay in, clean your sensor" a valid honest answer?
  • Wind & temperature: worth pulling for long-exposure stability / "dress warm," or scope creep?
  • Does conditions guidance feed the push copy (E3/N3) too, or stay in-app for now?
08

Build slices

Ordered so each slice stands on its own and the risky re-rank (D1) stays last:

  • Slice 1 — Conditions read. Extend the Open-Meteo fetch with precipitation_probability + weather_code; add the pure Conditions type + a conditionsFor(window) helper. Tested. (tiny)
  • Slice 2 — Gear-readiness. Pure kitReadiness(kit, phase) over maxAperture → verdict + honest line. Buildable today, no new data. Tested against the f-stop table.
  • Slice 3 — Shot-type rules. shotsFor(conditions, kit) → 1–3 kit-filtered shot prompts from the §04 table. Tested.
  • Slice 4 — Surface it (additive, D1). The "quiet night" hero gains a brief: readiness line + shot prompts + any rain warning. Wire into Today / Detail. This alone fixes the empty-list collapse.
  • Slice 5 — (later) weatherSealed in the catalog (D3 v2) → personalize the rain warning.
  • Slice 6 — (later, if earned) conditions re-rank (D1) → gray nights promote flat-light spots. Do it inside the one scorer, with Events.
Recommended first build

Slices 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 as one thread — the additive brief. It's self-contained, needs no ranking changes, uses data we already have (plus the trivial precip add), and turns the overcast "quiet night" into a shoot plan. Re-rank waits until it's earned.
